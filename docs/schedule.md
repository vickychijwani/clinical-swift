# Schedule

## Timeline and breaks

A break expands only for items that start inside it, not items that only cross
it, so long appointments do not expand the day unexpectedly. Compressed break
minutes keep proportional height so crossing items do not show misleading
durations.

In narrow overlap layouts, keep start times readable before patient names.

## Booking rules

Allow booking over appointments, breaks, and unavailable time: a visiting
dentist can work while the owner is busy or away. Suggest free working hours
because the clinic usually has one dentist, not as a save restriction.

Suggest a time only when a new booking session starts so opening a saved
booking, changing dates, or reloading the schedule cannot move the doctor's
chosen time.

Patient recency uses past appointments and patient creation; patient edits and
future bookings must not displace patients who need follow-up.
